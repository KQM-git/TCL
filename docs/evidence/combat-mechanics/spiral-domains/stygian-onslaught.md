### Onslaught No Bp

**By:** @alphaflyte  
**Added:** <Version date="2026-07-31" />  
**Last tested:** <VersionHl date="2025-07-02" />  
[Discussion](https://tickets.deeznuts.moe/transcripts/onslaught-no-bp)

**Finding:** Clearing a boss in Stygian Onslaught and claiming the artifact rewards using resin will *not* count towards BP progression for Bosses Defeated or Domains Cleared.

**Evidence:** [YouTube](https://youtu.be/pRZCHPTs10I)

**Significance:** BP enjoyers very sadge.

### Onslaught Friendship To Active Team

**By:** @.rainfury  
**Added:** <Version date="2025-08-16" />  
**Last tested:** <VersionHl date="2025-08-16" />  
[Discussion](https://tickets.deeznuts.moe/transcripts/onslaught-friendship-to-active-team)

**Finding:** Stygian Onslaught gives friendship exp to the team used to complete the battle and not the one in the overworld 

**Evidence:** [YouTube](https://youtu.be/y7qwgFaksYo) \
Dahlia is actively selected in the overworld \
Xilo is used in the team to fight the boss \
After claiming, Xilo friendship increased

**Significance:** Potentially amazing way to farm friendship by not using them doesn't work.
